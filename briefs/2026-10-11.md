## Regressions and risks

**1. Pricing Rule 2 is documented but not enforced, and a price just moved.**
`src/lib/products.ts` states two invariants in its header comment. Rule 1 (the
mentor session stays dearest) is checked at import time and throws on a Vercel
build. **Rule 2 (the bundle must cost more than either product it contains) has no
such guard.** Both rules currently hold — mentor $29 > dearest product $20; bundle
$20 > careers $18 and > CV $5 — but the bundle now clears the standalone careers
report by only **$2**, down from $11 before `5ac5d4c`. Any future edit that raises
`CAREERS_CENTS` past 2000 or drops `BUNDLE_CENTS` below it would ship a strictly
dominated product silently, on a repo with no tests and no local build. The fix is
five lines beside the existing check — a `throw` if
`BUNDLE_CENTS <= Math.max(CV_CENTS, CAREERS_CENTS)`. Not applied here: this routine
only writes to `briefs/`.

**2. Scoring was down for five days and nothing would have caught it.** Covered
above and now fixed by `781a448`. The risk that remains is structural: a model
migration described as parameter-free broke every paid and free path on the site,
and it was found by a human noticing, not by anything in the repo.

**3. Web Analytics has been off for four weeks while the beacon ships.**
`<Analytics />` at `src/app/layout.tsx:89`, `@vercel/analytics` at
`package.json:12`. No code fix needed and the `vercel/install-vercel-web-analytics-ev5kw0`
branch must not be merged (unrelated history; see *Sitting unmerged*).

**4. `/leads-dashboard` cannot see a buyer who never used a free tool.**
`src/lib/leads.ts` builds its list from the `fledgy:leads` set plus a `kv.keys()`
scan of exactly two prefixes, `fledgy:tools:*` and `fledgy:usage:*`. Both parse
correctly — `usage.ts:41` writes `fledgy:usage:<tool>:<email>` and `leads.ts:22`
writes `fledgy:tools:<email>`, and `addFromKey` takes the segment after the **last**
colon and requires an `@`, which reads both shapes. **No parsing break found.** But
nine other `fledgy:` families carry an email and are never scanned:
`fledgy:ent:` (entitlements — i.e. paying customers), `fledgy:report:`,
`fledgy:leadseen:`, `fledgy:unsub:`, `fledgy:unsub-token:`, `fledgy:autoreply:`,
`fledgy:autoreply:tool:<tool>:`, `fledgy:backfill:whatsnew:`, `fledgy:bonus:`,
`fledgy:referredby:`, `fledgy:referralpaid:`. All put the email last, so any of them
could be added to the scan safely. The one that matters is `fledgy:ent:` — moot only
because no charge has ever been taken.
