## Regressions and risks

**No code regressions found.** Every invariant checked this week holds.

- **Pricing rule 1 — mentor session dearest: HOLDS.** `MENTOR_CENTS` 2900 > dearest product
  `BUNDLE_CENTS` 2000. Enforced at import time by the `throw` at `products.ts`, which executed
  during this brief's verified build and did not fire.
- **Pricing rule 2 — bundle above its parts: HOLDS.** `BUNDLE_CENTS` 2000 > `CAREERS_CENTS` 1800
  and > `CV_CENTS` 500. Margin over the standalone careers report is **$2** — correct, but the
  thinnest of any week recorded. A $2 drop in the bundle or a $3 rise in careers makes the careers
  report strictly dominated. Worth knowing before the next price edit; not a problem now.
- **Paywall slicing rule: HOLDS in all three routes.** Each prompt asks for the full ranked answer
  and the route slices the free tier off the **end**, server-side:
  `api/essay/route.ts:149` `allTips.slice(-3)`; `api/cv/route.ts:149` `allTips.slice(-2)`;
  `api/careers/route.ts:222` `careers.slice(-1)`. All three prompts carry an explicit "Give the FULL
  review … never hold back here" / "ORDERED BEST-FIT FIRST … This ordering is load-bearing"
  instruction. No route asks the model for less.
- **`leads.ts` key parsing: INTACT.** It scans exactly two families, `fledgy:tools:*` and
  `fledgy:usage:*`, and takes the segment after the last colon. Live shapes are
  `fledgy:tools:<email>` (`leads.ts:22`) and `fledgy:usage:<tool>:<email>` (`usage.ts:41`) — the
  email is last in both, so both parse. The other eighteen `fledgy:*` families in `src/lib/`
  (`ent:`, `report:`, `stripe:session:`, `bonus:`, `referredby:`, `referralpaid:`, `leadseen:`,
  `lead:`, `autoreply:`, `autoreply:tool:`, `unsub:`, `unsub-token:`, `backfill:whatsnew:`) are
  **not** scanned, so none of them can inject garbage rows. No key gained or lost a segment this
  week.
- **No dependency change.** `git log --since='7 days ago' -p -- package.json package-lock.json`
  returned **nothing**, on any branch. `npm ci` left the lockfile byte-identical. The one
  irreversible build risk was not touched.
